## 1. Established proof-tier definitions

This is the exact certification hierarchy of **`YOU_ARE_GOODWORKFLOWFRONTIER` v1_0_15**, as defined in [`WORKFLOW.txt`](WORKFLOW.txt). The **whole mathematical statement**, not merely a similarly named helper, must meet the gate.

| Marker | Tier | Required proof standard |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| `!` | **B-Tier — derivation** | Mathematical proof worked out on paper, still conditional on correctness of its reasoning; **not** Lean certified. |
| `!!` | **A-Tier — kernel proof** | The **exact stated proposition** has parsed, elaborated and compiled under Lean 4.33.0 and passed the **full transitive axiom audit**, with no `sorryAx`, new custom axioms or concealed hypothesis. If the Lean theorem is conditional, only the conditional implication is proved. |
| `!!!` | **Formal closure** | An A-Tier conditional chain has reduced the **target** to accepting **finite raw witness data** in a terminating checker; the concrete witness has not yet passed. It is not S-Tier. |
| `!!!!` | **S-Tier — witness admitted** | An actual raw-data finite witness passes that checker, and the complete downstream Lean/audited transfer to the target theorem passes. |

**Partial ≠ B-Tier ≠ A-Tier:** a partially formalized printed theorem may have **multiple independently `!!`-proved subclauses**, while still lacking a genuine mathematical bridge. *Blocked* means a literal printed assertion is false or requires substantive correction; an A-Tier corrected statement does not prove that original assertion. **Nothing in this 45-item manuscript is presently certified as `!!!` or `!!!!`.**
